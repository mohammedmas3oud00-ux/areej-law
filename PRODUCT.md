# PRODUCT.md — Areej Al-Majnuni Law Firm (مكتب المحامية أريج بنت عويض المجنوني)

## 1. Product Truth & Mission
The official digital portal for **Advocate Areej Bint Aweid Al-Majnuni Law Firm**, an authorized and licensed Saudi legal practice (Ministry of Justice License #42691) headquartered in Makkah Al-Mukarramah (Al-Shawqiyyah).

The practice serves individuals, corporate clients, contractors, and public employees seeking high-stakes legal advocacy, judicial defense, Board of Grievances (ديوان المظالم) litigation, and contract structuring across the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia.

## 2. Target Audience & Personas
- **Corporate & Commercial Clients**: Businesses, institutional entities, and entrepreneurs requiring commercial contract drafting, labor dispute mediation, company bylaws, and courtroom litigation.
- **Government Contractors & Civil Servants**: Individuals and corporations challenging administrative decisions or tender disputes before the Board of Grievances (ديوان المظالم).
- **Private Citizens & Families**: Individuals needing urgent legal opinions, inheritance structuring, civil claims, or personal status legal guidance in Makkah and Western Region.

## 3. Brand Voice & Emotional Qualities
- **Dignified & Authoritative**: Grounded in the deep judicial traditions of Saudi law and Vision 2030 justice sector modernization.
- **Reassuring & Accessible**: Frictionless consultation booking (direct WhatsApp automation with structured briefs, in-person office visits in Al-Shawqiyyah).
- **Absolute Integrity & Discretion**: Client confidentiality, transparent working hours (morning and evening shifts), verified credentials, and documented peer reviews.

## 4. Key Conversion Paths
1. **Direct WhatsApp Structured Consultation**: Client fills brief (case details, shift preference, service type) generating an instant pre-filled WhatsApp message.
2. **In-Person Office Appointment**: Booking visit to the office (Al-Shawqiyyah, 2nd Floor, Office B9).
3. **Specialized Board of Grievances Inquiries**: Dedicated deep-dive into administrative law, decision nullifications, and compensation claims.
